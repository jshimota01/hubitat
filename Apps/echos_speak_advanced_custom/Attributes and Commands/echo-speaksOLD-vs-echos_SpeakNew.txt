Old Echo Speaks
Primary Speech & Announcement Commands:
    speak("text") — Basic text-to-speech
    playAnnouncement("text") / playAnnouncementAll("text") — Broadcasts an announcement
    playAnnouncementIncludeVis("text") — Announcement with visual display on Echo Shows
    sendCmdToToDevice("cmd", "val") — Raw command executor

Built-In Alexa Automation & Action Functions:
    executeRoutine("routine name") — Triggers an Alexa Routine
    playBuiltInSound("sound_name") — Plays built-in Alexa sound effects
    playFunFact() / playSingASong() / playTellJoke() — Trigger fun Alexa routines
    setDoNotDisturb(true/false) — Toggles DND mode
    setAlarm("time") / createReminder("text", "time") — Creates alarms/reminders

Media & Playback Controls:
    play(), pause(), stop(), nextTrack(), previousTrack() — Standard playback
    setVolume(0-100) / volumeUp() / volumeDown() — Volume adjustment
    playMusicProvider("search_term", "provider_name") — Search and stream music
    setBluetooth("device_name") / disconnectBluetooth() — Connect/disconnect Bluetooth
